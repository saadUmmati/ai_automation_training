CALCULUS ASSIGNMENT — SOLVED
Topic: Limits, Differentiation and Integration

1. LIMITS

(a) lim(x→2) (x²−4)/(x−2)
= lim(x→2) [(x−2)(x+2)/(x−2)]
= 2+2 = 4.

(b) lim(x→0) sin x/x = 1.

(c) lim(x→∞) (3x²+5x−1)/(x²−4)
Divide by x²:
= lim(x→∞) (3+5/x−1/x²)/(1−4/x²)
= 3.

2. DIFFERENTIATION

(a) y = 5x⁴−3x²+7x−9
dy/dx = 20x³−6x+7.

(b) y = (3x²+2x−1)⁵
Using the chain rule:
dy/dx = 5(3x²+2x−1)⁴(6x+2).

(c) y = sin x + cos x
dy/dx = cos x − sin x.

3. TANGENT LINE

Find the tangent to f(x)=x²+3x−4 at x=2.

f(2)=4+6−4=6.
f′(x)=2x+3, so f′(2)=7.

Using y−y₁=m(x−x₁):
y−6=7(x−2)
Therefore, y = 7x−8.

4. APPLICATION OF DERIVATIVES

f(x)=x³−6x²+9x+2

f′(x)=3x²−12x+9
=3(x−1)(x−3).

Critical points: x=1 and x=3.

f(1)=1−6+9+2=6.
f(3)=27−54+27+2=2.

Using the second derivative:
f″(x)=6x−12.

f″(1)=−6 < 0 → local maximum at (1,6).
f″(3)=6 > 0 → local minimum at (3,2).

5. INTEGRATION

(a) ∫(4x³−6x+2)dx
= x⁴−3x²+2x+C.

(b) ∫₀²(x²+3x+1)dx
= [x³/3 + 3x²/2 + x]₀²
= 8/3 + 6 + 2
= 32/3 square units.

(c) ∫2x(x²+1)⁴dx
Let u=x²+1, du=2x dx.
Therefore:
∫u⁴du = u⁵/5+C
= (x²+1)⁵/5+C.

6. AREA UNDER A CURVE

For y=x²+1 from x=0 to x=3:

Area = ∫₀³(x²+1)dx
= [x³/3+x]₀³
= 9+3
= 12 square units.

7. MOTION PROBLEM

s(t)=t³−6t²+9t+4.

Velocity:
v(t)=s′(t)=3t²−12t+9.

Acceleration:
a(t)=v′(t)=6t−12.

At t=2:
v(2)=12−24+9=−3 m/s.
a(2)=12−12=0 m/s².

8. CONCEPTUAL QUESTION

A derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change of a function, such as velocity being the rate of change of position.

An integral represents accumulation. A definite integral can be used to find quantities such as area or displacement.

CONCLUSION

Calculus provides mathematical tools for studying change and accumulation. Derivatives are mainly used for rates of change and optimization, while integrals are used for area, accumulation, and total change.
